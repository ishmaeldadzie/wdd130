Ghana

Ghana is a country in West Africa. It was colonized by the British up to 6th of March, 1957. The first president of Ghana was Doctor Kwame Nkrumah. Currently, our president is called his excellency John Dramani Mahama. The colors of flag are red, yellow and green. The red represents the blood and toil of our fathers.The yellow represents the mineral resources of the country whist the green represents the vegetable. The black star in the middle of flag represents the hope of Africa. I love my country because, most of the citizens are peaceful. This makes the county peaceful.
