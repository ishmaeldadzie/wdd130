History

God Hears Rafting Company started operation in April 29th, 2024. The idea to start a rifting company occurred to the CEO, Mr Ishmael Arkoh Dadzie when he visited a river side after a break up with his fiancee. The scenes at the river side that day made him forget the pains he was going through and decided to bring that same relief we normally go through as normal people.
